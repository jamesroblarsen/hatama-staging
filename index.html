      How to play

      Guess the five-letter Batak Toba word in five tries. Type a word and press Enter.

      • h right letter, right place
      • a in the word, wrong place
      • t not in the word

      Answers use only the 18 letters of Toba spelling. The faded keys (c, f, v, w, y, z) appear only in loanwords, which you may guess but which are never the answer.

      There is one word a day, the same for everyone, and a new one at midnight. When you're done you see the word in Batak script and what it means, and you can share your result.

      Every answer is checked by a Batak Toba speaker. About Hatama
